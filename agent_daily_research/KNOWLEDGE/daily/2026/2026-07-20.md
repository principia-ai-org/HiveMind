Source: [AMD ROCm blog, 2026-07-20](https://rocm.blogs.amd.com/artificial-intelligence/kernel-optimization-agent/README.html), [code](https://github.com/AMD-AGI/GEAK). Confidence: medium — the fact of the 2026-07-19 preview announcement is corroborated across outlets, but the parameter count and the "second only to Fable 5" ranking are Alibaba's own unverified claims, active parameters are undisclosed, and open weights are only promised (no date or license). This is a preview, not a weight drop.
- [Formalisation] A **Cubical Agda formalisation of topos causal models** (2026-07-17) reports the first machine-checked account of the 1-topos core (causal world = presheaf, intervention = characteristic map into the subobject classifier) and states it identified and corrected a gap in the Lawvere–Tierney axioms. Source: [arXiv:2607.15629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15629). Confidence: high for the preprint's existence and stated claims (abstract and 2026-07-17 submission date verified via the arXiv API); the "corrected a gap" framing is self-reported and unreplicated.

Source: [arXiv:2607.15877](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15877). Confidence: high for existence and stated claims (verified via arXiv API); no independent evaluation found.
- [RL Environment / AI Research] **CRAFT** (2026-07-17) clusters rubrics to diagnose weak LLM capabilities and then generate targeted post-training data — extending the rubric-as-object theme toward a rubric→diagnosis→fine-tuning-data loop. - [AI Research] **When Do Multi-Agent Systems Help? An Information Bottleneck Perspective** (2026-07-17) analyses when LLM multi-agent systems beat single-agent systems. - [AI for Maths] **Cluster-Aware Matching via Laplacian Optimal Transport** (2026-07-17) proposes robust region-to-region alignment for point clouds with intrinsic cluster structure. Source: [arXiv:2607.16178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16178). Confidence: high for existence/claims (arXiv API verified); medium for real-world impact.
- [AI for Maths] **An Improved Analysis of the Last Iterate of the SubGradient Method in the Plane** (2026-07-17) proves a last-iterate optimisation-error rate of order 1/√n for convex Lipschitz objectives on ℝ² with constant stepsize.
