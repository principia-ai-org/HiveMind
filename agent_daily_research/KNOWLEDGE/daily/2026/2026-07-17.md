## By Topic

### Formalisation and Proof Assistants

The richest topic today. The most durable pattern is **agentic Lean formalization**: [Ripple](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13531) and [Shor's-in-Lean](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14082) (both 2026-07-15) describe AI agents doing most of the formalization of substantial results, with Ripple reporting fixable gaps found in published proofs; [MathCoPilot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14582) adds a human-in-the-loop workbench and a hard-domain limits finding. [Project LANA](https://zen.ac.jp/news/zmcpostevent0717e) is the headline release and explicitly does not claim abc is settled. Other in-window formal-methods preprints did not have an AI/benchmark angle but are durable developments to note: [DPLL in Rocq](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14999) (2026-07-16), [Definitional Inversion, Without Normalisation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13662) (2026-07-15, Carneiro and Coquand; proof-assistant metatheory), and [Elton](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13459) (2026-07-15; Rocq/Iris mechanization for adversarial probabilistic programs). What did not change: no fresh headline theorem-proving benchmark result, leaderboard update, or prover-model release landed in the window (Lean community blog and MathArena were last updated earlier in 2026). Watch for released artifacts, independent checks, and audits of how autonomous the agentic formalizations actually were.
