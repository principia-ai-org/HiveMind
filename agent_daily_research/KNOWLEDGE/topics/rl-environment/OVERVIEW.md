# RL Environment

## Short Summary

Track RL environments for training and evaluating models, with emphasis on judging and grading criteria (reward design, rubrics, verifiers, auto-graders) and the kinds of tasks environments focus on, across both market demand and technical progress.

## Durable Takeaways

- 2026-07-17: A cluster of 2026-07-15/16 preprints treats the grader/rubric itself as an object to be auto-generated, validated, and stress-tested for robustness (Rubrics on Trial; When Rubrics Change; Alipay-PIBench). Evidence is preprint-level and needs replication. See [Rubric and Verifier Design Emerges as an RL-Environment Focus](notes/2026-07-17-rubric-verifier-design.md).
- 2026-07-20: The rubric-as-object theme is extending from robustness toward a diagnosis-and-data loop: [CRAFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16122) (2026-07-17) clusters rubrics to diagnose weak model capabilities and then generate targeted post-training data. Single primary preprint, no replication. See [2026-07-20 daily](../../daily/2026/2026-07-20.md).
